TIGER EMPIRE FOOTBALL ACADEMY — PLAYER REGISTRATION UPDATE

FILES INCLUDED
- index.html: updated homepage with Register a Player links
- registration.html: player registration form
- thank-you-registration.html: confirmation page after submission
- thank-you.html: existing general enquiry confirmation page

DEPLOY
1. Upload/deploy the complete contents of this folder to the existing Netlify site. Keep all files together in the published site root.
2. In the Netlify site dashboard, open Forms and enable form detection if it is not already enabled.
3. Trigger a fresh deploy after enabling form detection so Netlify can detect the player-registration form.
4. After publishing, open /registration.html and submit a clearly labelled test application using your own test details.
5. In Netlify Forms, check that the submission appears under the player-registration form before sharing the registration link publicly.

IMPORTANT
- This is a basic online application form, not a full player database or club-management system.
- The form collects personal information, including information about children. Limit dashboard access, only use information for registration/contact, and follow applicable privacy and child-protection rules.
- Submitting an application does not confirm acceptance or a trial date.
